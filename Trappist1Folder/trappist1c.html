TRAPPIST - 1C

Description

TRAPPIST-1c is the second planet from its host star, similar to Earth in size and mass. Receiving radiation comparable to Venus, it features a heavily heated, rocky surface with a surface temperature around 340 K. Observations suggest a bare surface or at most a very thin atmosphere, making conditions far too harsh for liquid water to endure.
