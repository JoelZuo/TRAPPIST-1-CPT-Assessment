SPECULOOS - 1

Description

SPECULOOS-1 (also known as TRAPPIST-1) is the ultra-cool dwarf star anchoring the entire planetary system. Composed primarily of plasma and packing nearly 30,000 Earth masses into a volume just larger than Jupiter, this ultra-cool red dwarf burns at a scorching 2,566 K. Its extreme radiation and lack of a solid surface make habitability or liquid water completely impossible.
